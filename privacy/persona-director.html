Privacy Policy

Persona Director is developed, operated, and published by URSUS Loft.

Persona Director does not operate user accounts or developer-operated servers. The app does not transmit character settings, prompts, presets, session data, Persona Notes, or other user-created configuration data to URSUS Loft or another external server.

Character settings, presets, and session-related data are stored locally on the user's device. Removing local application data may remove saved presets and session information. Windows removal or uninstall behavior can vary, so complete removal of locally stored application data is not guaranteed.

Persona Director generates and formats character prompts locally based on the settings selected by the user. Persona Director does not automatically send generated prompts or character information to external AI services. If the user manually copies or submits generated content to a third-party AI service, that service processes the submitted data under its own privacy policy and terms.

Persona Director does not include advertising, third-party analytics tracking, behavioral profiling, or the sale of personal information.

Microsoft Store, Windows, and any external service independently selected by the user may process data under their own policies. URSUS Loft does not control those services or how those services process data.

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@ursusloft.com.
