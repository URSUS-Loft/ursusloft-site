Privacy Policy

Scene Director is developed, operated, and published by URSUS Loft.

Scene Director does not operate user accounts or developer servers. The app does not transmit reference images, prompts, presets, session data, or project settings to URSUS Loft or another external server. Reference images are selected from the user's local device for local preview and prompt organization.

Last-session and preset data are stored locally under:
%APPDATA%\Scene Director
These files store text and settings, including reference-image roles. They do not store reference image files, filenames, or file paths. Deleting the local files removes those saved session or preset settings. Windows removal or uninstall behavior can vary, so complete removal of AppData files is not guaranteed.

Microsoft Store, Windows, and any external AI service selected by the user process data under their own policies. URSUS Loft does not control these services or how those services process data.

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@ursusloft.com.
