URSUS Link

Share files and folders directly from your PC.

Get it on Microsoft Store

Share the folder you choose with a simple link. URSUS Link helps you send photos, videos, project files, and large files directly from your Windows PC—without first uploading copies to a separate cloud-storage service.

Getting started is simple: select a folder, start sharing, and send the generated link and required four-digit PIN to your recipient. They can browse and download the shared files in a supported web browser. They do not need to install or purchase URSUS Link.

Files travel directly from your PC to the recipient's browser over HTTPS; the original files are not stored on a separate file-hosting server. URSUS Link is suitable for sharing large files, but actual transfer speed and workable file size depend on your connection, browser, computer, and available storage. Keep the sending PC powered on and online while sharing. Pay once—no subscription.

Before you buy: network compatibility
Your network must allow external HTTPS connections to reach the sending PC. Some routers, firewalls, ISP restrictions, double-NAT, or carrier-grade NAT (CGNAT) setups may prevent this. If automatic setup fails and you cannot change the router or network configuration, sharing over the internet may not work. URSUS Link does not relay files through a server to guarantee connectivity on every network. Check that your network supports direct connections before purchasing.
